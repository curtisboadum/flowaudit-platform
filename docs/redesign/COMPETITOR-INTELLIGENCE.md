# Competitive intelligence and source coverage

Research began in the planning phase with browser interaction, desktop/mobile inspection and source reads. This is a record of observations and interpretation, not an independent verification of competitors’ business results. No competitor form or booking was submitted.

## Bosar

Observed on [the homepage](https://www.bosar.agency/): a practical transformation proposition, restrained paper/dark art direction, Manrope and Source Serif 4, real people/work imagery, service groupings and audit-to-adoption process. The navigation expands into operational solution pages. Visible work examples have contextual journeys rather than decoration alone.

Interpretation: buyers can connect an abstract AI offer to familiar work and see who owns delivery. It can fail when broad transformation copy demands too much inference from a buyer seeking one immediate solution. FlowAudit therefore puts a real dental call beside its broad proposition and retains distinct service paths.

Observed on [JB Trenchless](https://www.bosar.agency/work/jb-trenchless/): AUD322K/year is presented as recoverable gross-profit opportunity, associated with an audit/blueprint. It must not be described as an achieved result. Process mapping and ownership make the proposed operational change understandable. FlowAudit uses recorded behaviour as evidence and does not borrow the estimate or imply equivalent outcomes.

Observed on [Quotation Factory](https://www.bosar.agency/work/quotation-factory/): team capability is presented through specific roles and interactive department views. Inputs, outputs and human decisions make the change tangible. This works because the interface explains how work changes; it could fail if readers mistake an illustrative interaction for live product proof. FlowAudit’s explorer explicitly explains a recorded sequence.

Observed on [contact](https://www.bosar.agency/contact/): substantial qualification asks for business context including revenue and team size before the next step. This can protect delivery capacity but adds friction. FlowAudit requests priority and role with optional dental context, and provides a direct-calendar bypass.

Source inspection in planning found a static frontend, dedicated scroll/motion code, mobile media provisions, reduced-motion handling and an Umami reference. These observations do not establish analytics configuration, backend security or actual performance under traffic. Motion can support orientation; it becomes costly when long scroll sequences delay comprehension. FlowAudit uses restrained transitions, native interactions and user-initiated media.

Coverage: planning discovered roughly 140 indexed URLs, many SEO articles, plus the core solution, work, process and conversion pages. Runtime interactions were sampled on key journeys; every possible interaction was not tested. During the implementation refresh, the homepage was reachable in-browser but `/sitemap.xml` returned NOT_FOUND. The fresh machine inventory therefore does not falsely claim to contain every Bosar page.

## LeftClick

Observed on [the homepage](https://leftclick.ai): direct founder-led positioning, DM Sans and mono accents, assertive proof claims, prominent booking and a tighter commercial narrative. This reduces uncertainty about the next step but depends heavily on trust in the named founder and the presented proof. FlowAudit cannot borrow those statistics or testimonials.

Observed on pricing during planning: a project minimum around $5K, typical $10–50K range and optional ongoing management. These are competitor-published commercial cues, not FlowAudit’s validated economics. An anchor screens buyers and communicates scope, but can hide meaningful differences in usage, integration and maintenance costs. FlowAudit uses a scoped quote until its cost assumptions are verified.

Observed in the booking path: Cal.com, a 30-minute meeting, and qualification covering interest, website, budget, monthly revenue and notes alongside contact details. A real available slot and field sequence were inspected without submission. FlowAudit retains its existing 15-minute Cal event and focuses the agenda on demo, compatibility, scope and next decision.

Published case studies span several client contexts. Dental Media Corp is a marketing-agency example and must not be mistaken for evidence of deployed clinical practice automation. Published outcomes were not independently verified. This is why FlowAudit’s proof architecture labels its exact recording and reserves future case-study slots for sourced evidence.

Fresh source inventory checked 26 LeftClick sitemap URLs successfully, recording titles, headings, form counts, scripts and response sizes in `competitor-inventory.json`. This is source coverage, not a claim of exhaustive runtime QA, accessibility compliance, analytics absence or backend inspection.

## Principles adopted

Specific evidence beside a clear offer; business-shaped explanations; visible human decisions; distinct qualification and scope; real people; readable editorial composition; a consistent booking action. Original FlowAudit typography, tokens, copy and layouts implement these principles. No competitor visual asset, testimonial, result, certification or proprietary source was copied.

## What remains uncertain

Neither public-source inspection nor browser use establishes private CRM logic, actual conversion rates, profit, traffic, security, legal compliance or current client satisfaction. No superiority or conversion-lift claim is made from these observations. Test FlowAudit’s funnel after deployment and improve from its own qualified outcomes.
