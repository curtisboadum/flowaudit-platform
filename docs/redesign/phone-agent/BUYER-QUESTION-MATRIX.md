## Sales preparation, private commercial boundaries

Use the matrix to prepare answers and identify gaps before the call. Do not treat “to agree” as a positive capability. For each quote, settle connector scope, call volume/overage, locations, recording/data requirements, acceptance tests, support hours, change requests, term and exit rights. Collect actual delivery/carrier/model/support costs privately before establishing margins. Unknown requirements are conditions to resolve before an agreement can proceed, not objections to dismiss.
