# Strategy and implementation record · 9 October 2026

The approved direction combines a broad agency with a concrete dental flagship. A dental-only homepage would make the existing operations, recovery and website services harder to discover. A generic “AI transformation” homepage would bury the working product. The implemented hero makes the operational promise broad, while the real call recording supplies specificity immediately.

The core commercial sequence is “See it work. Approve it. Then activate it.” This means approval before activation, not universally free configuration. Phone agreement and initial payment start configuration. Website work offers a bounded custom demo before payment, followed by a 12-month managed agreement. Public pages use scoped quotes; private price experiments are separate local notes.

## Proof register

The routine 90.8-second recording shows a conversation, offered times and Google Calendar booking. It does not establish a production dental deployment or universal PMS integration. Privacy beeps and edited labels are disclosed. The full VSL contains an urgent-question excerpt: it does not establish clinical validation or completed emergency escalation. Finished main, executive, teaser and routine media were integrated from the separate production handoff. Automated film QA passed; human listening/viewing approval remains pending.

No public testimonials, recovered-revenue claims, clinical assurances, certifications, universal tool logos or guaranteed outcomes were added. Industry examples are labelled illustrative. Procurement and wider group rollout require further verified assessment.

## Measurement and rollout

Compare unique consented journeys by source/service: evidence engagement → booking click → calendar readiness. Signed server booking events remain operational truth once configured; a booking click is not a booking. Meeting-end provider events do not establish attendance, revenue or a sale. Keep calls qualified without an unnecessary video gate. Decide price experiments from actual delivery costs, fit-call quality and retained contribution, not from conversion rate alone.

Release sequence: review concrete preview and films, finish Cal signing configuration, restore the separately owned client app, repeat production smoke tests, then promote the reviewed commit. Lab performance and automated accessibility reports are in evidence; field CWV requires deployed traffic. No live-domain promotion is implied by a preview deployment.
