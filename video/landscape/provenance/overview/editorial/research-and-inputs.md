## Evidence informing the treatment

- [Cambridge multimedia-learning research](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-handbook-of-multimedia-learning/principles-for-reducing-extraneous-processing-in-multimedia-learning-coherence-signaling-redundancy-spatial-contiguity-and-temporal-contiguity-principles/CD5B7AE1279A9AB81F8EEBB53DBEC86E) supports reducing irrelevant material and synchronizing relevant words and pictures. This supports selective text and meaningful motion; it does not establish a sales-conversion uplift.
- [W3C prerecorded captions guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/captions-prerecorded.html) informs accessible dialogue text. Mobile readability was checked in the actual player; source recording resolution remains a limit.
- [ADA staffing research](https://www.ada.org/resources/research/health-policy-institute/dentist-workforce/dental-hygienist-shortage) provides context for staffing pressure. It does not prove reception-call losses for the lead list.
- [ADA AI attitudes research](https://www.ada.org/resources/research/health-policy-institute/dental-practice-research/dentists-ai-usage-and-attitudes) concerns dental AI adoption more broadly, including clinical use. It is not direct evidence of phone-agent purchase intent.
- [BDA AI overview](https://www.bda.org/advice/your-dental-business/data-and-privacy/using-artificial-intelligence-ai-in-dental-practice/) highlights data/privacy considerations. Only the accessible overview was read; member-only material was unavailable.
- [Wistia video research discussion](https://wistia.com/blog/state-of-video-webinar-recap) is observational context for video attention, not a guarantee of this film’s performance.
