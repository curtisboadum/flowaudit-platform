# FlowAudit: bold motion explainer

## Creative direction

Build a roughly 90-second film in which **graphic objects and their transformations carry the argument**. No character acting. The emotional progression is recognisable pressure → practical relief → genuine evidence → confidence in staff control → invitation.

Use crisp 2D geometry, strong contrast, oversized meaningful objects and restrained depth. Start with ink, white and cobalt; use coral for unresolved pressure and green sparingly for resolution. Avoid the rejected film’s illustrated-room compositions, small diagrams, repeated entrances and large narration text.

Make the motion ambitious through connected transformations, masks, changing scale, controlled overshoot and motivated camera moves. Each flourish must reveal a relationship or change the situation. Slow down around genuine evidence.
