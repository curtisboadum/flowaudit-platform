# Original score and sound provenance

The score is an original, algorithmically composed 32-second instrumental loop created for this FlowAudit production. Its source is create_score.py in the editable package. It uses additive sine synthesis, open D/B minor/G/A voicings and an original sparse bell sequence. No sampled recordings, stock library, external melody or paid service was used. All audio is synthesized locally at 48kHz.

The visual sample uses this bed and mutes it completely during its demo excerpt. The completed evidence exports contain no added music or SFX. Final narrated-film scoring awaits the voice decision. Minimal transition SFX use an original locally synthesized breath of filtered noise; no SFX during source calls.
